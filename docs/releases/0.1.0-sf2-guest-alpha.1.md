# Syphon Filter 2 PC - Guest Runtime Alpha 1

This is an experimental Windows x64 testing package for the retail
*Syphon Filter 2* guest runtime. It is not a complete sequel port.

## Download

- `SyphonFilterPC-0.1.0-sf2-guest-alpha.1-win64.zip`
- `SyphonFilterPC-0.1.0-sf2-guest-alpha.1-win64.zip.sha256`
- Verify the ZIP with the SHA-256 sidecar before extraction.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11 x64.
- A legally obtained *Syphon Filter 2* USA Disc 1 BIN/CUE image.
- Every BIN track named by the CUE must remain beside it.
- A GPU and driver supporting OpenGL 3.x.

No disc image, save, settings or extracted retail asset is included.

## Installation and launch

1. Extract the entire ZIP into a new writable directory.
2. Open PowerShell in that extracted directory.
3. Create the required empty developer marker:

   ```powershell
   New-Item -ItemType File -Path .\syphon_filter_cheats
   ```

4. Launch the validated Mission 3 path, substituting your CUE location:

   ```powershell
   .\syphon_filter.exe `
     --no-launcher --mission=3 --scene-test `
     "D:\PS1\Syphon Filter 2 (USA) (Disc 1).cue"
   ```

The integrated graphical launcher is still the SF1 product launcher and does
not expose this experimental SF2 runtime.

## Validated behavior

- Mission 3 retail in-engine introduction and gameplay handoff.
- Keyboard, mouse and controller input.
- Third-person movement, strafing, crouch, climbing and door interaction.
- Lock-on combat, first-person mouse aim, weapon pickup and switching.
- XA dialogue, SPU sound effects, death and retail checkpoint restart.
- Partial native HUD projection.

## Known limitations

- Mission 3 is the only validated mission.
- Some knife and pistol HUD artwork is absent.
- Campaign transitions, saving, music and other missions are incomplete.
- A later scripted conversation may remain locked after its final line.
- Streamed-room presentation can flicker at medium range, with surfaces
  appearing and disappearing even while the camera is stationary.
- Guest collision residency can fail during traversal and allow Gabe to fall
  through the floor and die.
- The latest deep-room texture residency correction is experimental and is not
  yet considered validated.

## Reporting issues

Include:

- Your approximate location and actions immediately before the problem.
- The last dialogue line heard, if relevant.
- A screenshot for presentation defects.
- The generated log beside the executable.

Never upload or attach BIN/CUE files or other copyrighted game data.
