# Syphon Filter 2 PC - Guest Runtime Alpha 2

This is an experimental Windows x64 testing package for the retail
*Syphon Filter 2* guest runtime. It is not a complete sequel port.

## Download

- `SyphonFilterPC-0.1.0-sf2-guest-alpha.2-win64.zip`
- `SyphonFilterPC-0.1.0-sf2-guest-alpha.2-win64.zip.sha256`
- Verify the ZIP with the SHA-256 sidecar before extraction.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11 x64.
- A legally obtained *Syphon Filter 2* USA Disc 1 BIN/CUE image.
- Every BIN track named by the CUE must remain beside it.
- A GPU and driver supporting OpenGL 3.x.

No disc image, save, settings or extracted retail asset is included.

## Installation and launch

1. Extract the entire ZIP into a new writable directory.
2. Open PowerShell in that extracted directory.
3. Create the required empty developer marker:

   ```powershell
   New-Item -ItemType File -Path .\syphon_filter_cheats
   ```

4. Launch Mission 3, substituting your CUE location:

   ```powershell
   .\syphon_filter.exe `
     --no-launcher --mission=3 --scene-test `
     "D:\PS1\Syphon Filter 2 (USA) (Disc 1).cue"
   ```

The graphical launcher remains the SF1 product launcher and does not expose
this experimental SF2 runtime.

## Validated behavior

- Mission 3 completes from its retail in-engine opening through the save menu
  and following cinematic.
- Retail choreography, dialogue, objectives, combat, doors, climbing,
  executions, weapons, death and checkpoint restart.
- Keyboard, mouse and controller input, including strafing, crouching,
  first-person mouse aim and directional weapon selection.
- XA dialogue, SPU sound effects and coherent audio after restart.
- Health/armor, ammunition, weapon icons, reticles and gameplay notices.
- In-session F5 quick save and F9 quick load.

## Controls added for SF2 testing

- `C`: crouch.
- `A` / `D`: strafe.
- Mouse movement: free look; both axes aim in first person.
- Mouse wheel or `[` / `]`: previous/next owned weapon.
- Middle mouse: advance one weapon.
- Number keys: select the corresponding owned weapon slot.
- `F5` / `F9`: in-session quick save/load. Quick states do not persist after
  the process exits.

## Known limitations

- Mission 3 is the only interactively completed mission.
- Collision/room residency can intermittently fail and allow Gabe to fall
  through the floor. Alpha 2 adds a focused room-14 request guard which needs
  broader testing.
- The Chance dialogue about the truck/lights can pause unevenly or remain
  locked; press `C` to skip it.
- HUD initialization can lag several seconds after gameplay begins or a
  checkpoint reload.
- A few UI atlas regions may still appear incomplete or incorrect.
- Music is absent and first-person mouse sensitivity needs tuning.
- F5/F9 presentation may expose experimental framebuffer defects.

## Reporting issues

Include:

- Your approximate room/location and actions immediately before the problem.
- Whether crouching, moving backward, switching weapons, or F5/F9 was involved.
- The last dialogue line heard, if relevant.
- A screenshot for presentation defects.
- The generated log beside the executable.

Never upload or attach BIN/CUE files or other copyrighted game data.
